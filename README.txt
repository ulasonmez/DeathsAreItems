================================================================================
DEATHS ARE ITEMS
Minecraft 1.20.1 | Forge 47.1.3
================================================================================

Discover 14 different ways to die and turn each first death into a powerful item.
Build your loadout before entering The End, where your adventure becomes a
personal Hardcore challenge.

Required supporting mods: none beyond Forge.


1. GET READY
------------
Use Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.1.3. Extract deathsareitems-1.0.0.jar from
the download ZIP and put it in your Forge profile's mods folder. In multiplayer,
install the same mod on the server and on every player's client.

Start a Survival world. Choose a wide, flat, open respawn area with solid ground
and space around you for three reward chests.

IMPORTANT: Collect your rewards before your first trip to The End. Entering
The End activates personal End Hardcore permanently until an operator resets
it. From that point onward, death locks you into Spectator mode, even if you
have left The End. Reward chests also stop appearing after End Hardcore begins.


2. EARN AND CLAIM YOUR FIRST REWARD
----------------------------------
Die from fall damage, then respawn and wait for the three reward chests to
finish appearing. Right-click the chest offering Fall Damage Boots to claim
your item. The other two choices are previews and cannot be claimed.

Equip the boots in your feet slot. They prevent fall damage. Landing creates
a 3x3 cushion that cycles through water, slime and hay. Sneak while landing to
launch breakable ground blocks into the air as flying debris.

Use the boots for safer exploration while setting up your other death attempts.

Each supported death type unlocks its reward only the first time that player
discovers it. Claim the offered item before attempting another death. Repeating
an already discovered death does not give another reward.

The route below is a suggested order. You can discover the remaining death
types in any order before End Hardcore begins. Store your gear safely before
each attempt. Some rewards protect you from their matching damage, so remove
or put away protective items if you deliberately want to repeat a death.

If chests do not appear, move to open, level ground and wait. If a reward is
still missing, see IF YOU GET STUCK at the end.


3. EXPLORE THE WATER - DROWNING MASK
----------------------------------
Drown underwater to earn the Drowning Mask. Wear it in your head slot for Water
Breathing, Dolphin's Grace and Conduit Power.

With the mask equipped and both hands empty, hold and release right-click to
throw a trident that inflicts a drowning attack on a target mob. Use the mask
for underwater exploration and the trident for ranged attacks.


4. CONTROL BLOCKS AND MOBS - SUFFOCATION BOULDER
---------------------------------------------
Die by suffocating inside solid blocks to earn the Suffocation Boulder.

Hold it in your main hand and right-click a block to turn a spherical area
into sand or gravel after three seconds. Right-click or hit a mob to trap it
inside a suffocating sand or gravel cage. Use it to reshape an area or stop
an ordinary mob while you prepare your next move.


5. GET A RANGED WEAPON - SKELETON BOW
----------------------------------
Let a skeleton kill you with an arrow to earn the Skeleton Bow.

Hold right-click to fire a rapid stream of arrows without carrying ammunition.
Use it to fight from a distance as you gather supplies for later encounters.


6. TURN MINING INTO ENCOUNTERS - MOB PICKAXE
-----------------------------------------
Let an iron golem kill you to earn the Mob Pickaxe.

Right-click to toggle mob spawning. When enabled, mining a block spawns a
random mob in its place. Keep enough room to react: the result may be hostile.
Toggle spawning off when you want to mine normally. The pickaxe also deals
40 melee damage with a fully charged hit.


7. BUILD A FOOD RESERVE - HUNGER STAFF
-----------------------------------
Die from starvation to earn the Hunger Staff. Let your hunger bar empty and
wait for starvation damage to finish the attempt. This mod allows starvation
to become fatal on Easy and Normal as well as Hard; use a non-Peaceful world.

Hold the staff in your main hand and right-click a mob to start draining it.
Over five seconds the target shrinks and supplies 10 food points. Stay nearby
until the food finishes reaching you. Excess food fills an extra reserve bar,
up to 100 food points. The reserve refills your normal hunger bar as needed
and slowly decreases while that bar is full.

Fill your reserve before longer exploration and dangerous encounters.


8. CALL DOWN ANVILS - SWARM OF ANVILS
-----------------------------------
Let a falling anvil kill you to earn Swarm of Anvils.

Hold it in your main hand and right-click a block or mob to rain anvils for
15 seconds. Mobs are pinned during the attack. At the end, a targeted block
drops with Infinity 255; mob drops receive random level-255 enchantments.
Use it for powerful attacks and unusual enchanted loot.


9. OVERWHELM A MOB - ENTITY CRAMMER
---------------------------------
Die from entity cramming by being packed into a small space with too many
entities to earn the Entity Crammer. Your world must allow cramming damage.

Right-click a mob to trap it in a glass cage that repeatedly clones it until
cramming kills the trapped mobs, then bursts outward. Leave room around the
target for the effect.


10. ADD DEFENSE - CACTUS CHESTPLATE
---------------------------------
Die from cactus damage to earn the Cactus Chestplate.

Wear it in your chest slot for 6 armor points. When a mob attacks you, cactus
spikes automatically retaliate, pinning the attacker and damaging it every
half second. Use it for protection during close-range encounters.


11. BLAST AND ROCKET-JUMP - TNT CANNON
------------------------------------
Die in an explosion from primed TNT to earn the TNT Cannon.

Right-click to fire primed TNT along an arc. It explodes on impact or after
three seconds. Shoot downward to rocket-jump without blast damage from your
own shot. Use the cannon for explosive attacks and the jump to gain height.


12. TRAVEL THROUGH HEAT - FIRE PANTS
----------------------------------
Die from lava damage to earn Fire Pants.

Wear them in your leg slot for 5 armor points and Fire Resistance. Sprint to
build up to 150% extra movement speed. While running, you leave an obsidian
trail that melts into lava after 1.5 seconds. Keep that trail away from places
you want to preserve. The pants also help you travel safely across lava.


13. MAKE A REUSABLE EXPLOSION - BEDROCK
-------------------------------------
Die from an exploding bed or respawn anchor to earn Bedrock. A bed explodes
when used in the Nether or The End; use the Nether for this discovery so you
do not start End Hardcore early. A charged respawn anchor explodes when used
outside the Nether.

Place Bedrock like a bed and right-click it. It produces a powerful, fiery
explosion every 1.5 seconds without harming you or destroying Bedrock itself.
Choose the location carefully before using it.


14. TELEPORT AND RETALIATE - ENDER PEARL SHIELD
--------------------------------------------
Die from an ender pearl's teleport damage to earn the Ender Pearl Shield.
The teleport damage itself must finish the attempt; landing somewhere and
dying from another hazard is a different death.

Hold the shield and right-click to throw a safe ender pearl without teleport
damage. If a mob attacks you while you hold the shield, it retaliates with
60 pearls that repeatedly teleport and damage the attacker. Use safe throws
for movement and keep the shield ready when mobs close in.


15. COLLECT SOULS - SOUL STORM
----------------------------
Die from the Wither effect to earn Soul Storm. The effect must deal the final
damage; a direct hit from a wither skeleton is not the same death type.

Sneak and hold right-click to harvest souls from nearby mobs, damaging and
shrinking them. Right-click without sneaking to fire an explosive soul
projectile. It deals heavy damage and can break obsidian. Gather souls before
using the projectile in a fight.


16. TAKE YOUR FINISHED LOADOUT TO THE END
---------------------------------------
Once you have claimed all 14 death rewards, your death collection is complete.
Choose your equipment, prepare supplies and finish any remaining death
discoveries before entering The End.

Your first entry changes your hearts to Hardcore hearts and activates personal
End Hardcore. No more death reward chests appear while it is active. Dying
after this point locks you into Spectator mode across dimensions, respawns and
server restarts. Leaving The End does not remove the challenge, and none of
your reward items can revive you.

Use your finished loadout for the End adventure and survive. If you need to
undo the death lock, an operator can use the recovery commands below.


IF YOU GET STUCK
----------------
These are optional cheats. They require cheats enabled in singleplayer or
operator permission level 2 on a server. Run self-targeting commands in-game.
Normal reward collection does not require commands.

Missing reward or no chest after a discovery:
  First move to a wide, flat, open area. To create the fall reward chest
  sequence again without dying, run:
    /deathsareitems preview fall
  Wait for the chests to settle, then claim the boots. Despite its name, this
  command creates a claimable reward offer. It replaces your current offer;
  it does not reset your discovery history.

Want to repeat one discovery:
    /deathsareitems reset fall
  This reopens only your fall discovery, without removing rewards you already
  own. Remove the boots and die from fall damage again to earn another offer.
  Alternatively, use the following cheat to force the matching death even if
  an item protects you:
    /deathsareitems die fall
  This kills you. Do not use it after entering The End unless you intend to
  trigger the End Hardcore death lock.

Want to restart all death discoveries:
    /deathsareitems reset all
  This resets your history for all 14 death types so they can be discovered
  again. It does not remove existing reward items or reset End Hardcore.

Locked in Spectator mode after End Hardcore:
    /deathsareitems revive @s
  This clears your End Hardcore status and death lock and returns a locked
  Spectator player to Survival. Your hearts return to normal. Entering The End
  again activates the challenge again.
  An operator can target another player instead:
    /deathsareitems revive <player>
  Replace <player> with that player's name. This targeted form also works from
  the server console. Reviving does not reset death discoveries.

Need a replacement item directly:
    /give @s deathsareitems:fall_damage_boots
  This gives the boots without a death or chest sequence. It does not change
  your discovery history or End Hardcore status.

For preview, reset and die, replace fall with the matching death type:
  fall, drown, suffocate, skeleton, golem, starve, anvil, cram, cactus,
  tnt, lava, bed, pearl, wither

For a direct /give replacement, use the matching item ID:
  fall       -> fall_damage_boots
  drown      -> drowning_mask
  suffocate  -> suffocation_boulder
  skeleton   -> skeleton_bow
  golem      -> mob_pickaxe
  starve     -> hunger_staff
  anvil      -> swarm_of_anvils
  cram       -> entity_crammer
  cactus     -> cactus_chestplate
  tnt        -> tnt_cannon
  lava       -> fire_pants
  bed        -> bedrock
  pearl      -> ender_pearl_shield
  wither     -> soul_storm
